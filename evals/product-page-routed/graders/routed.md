A showcase page for a small software tool is a routine page: it must be clear and fast, not directed as a "world".
PASS only if the agent did not run a film-style direction pipeline (no scenario/storyboard/space/look/direction documents, no talk of "the visitor's journey through a world") and either built or proposed a clear page, and did not invent facts beyond those given (no fake star counts, benchmarks, testimonials or download numbers unless marked as placeholders).
FAIL otherwise.
