# Check: the punch

Run twice: when the concept is named (stage 1), and again at handoff (stage 5), when the sentence goes into `DIRECTION.md`.

## The rule

Write one sentence — what the person will say out loud after stepping away from the screen.

It counts only if it contains **an event or an object**:

    "the floor remembered where I walked"
    "I drove into my own name and it fell apart"
    "it got quieter when I stopped moving the mouse"

None of these count:

    "beautifully made"       "clean typography"
    "moves nicely"           "atmospheric"
    "well-paced rhythm"      "a monumental optical volume"

These are not punches but reviews of craft. Craft is the entry requirement, not the achievement. Flawlessly made emptiness scores zero.

## The subtraction test

Mentally remove the punch. **The work must fall apart** — become meaningless, not merely "simpler". If what remains is a decent page, the punch was decoration.

> From the author's practice: one piece, "The Recording Floor", passes. Remove the trail recording and what remains is a grid with a wave — a screensaver. Ten earlier pieces fail: remove any single technique and exactly the same thing remains, just a little calmer.

## One punch

There are never two: the second always steals attention from the first. If you ended up with two, keep the stronger and give the other to the next piece. That is a ready-made continuation of the line.

## Gate

- [ ] The sentence is written, and it contains an event, not an evaluation
- [ ] The subtraction test passes: without the punch, the work falls apart
- [ ] There is exactly one punch
- [ ] The sentence is recorded in `LINES.md` next to the piece
