# Check: the punch

The one event the visitor will retell. Run twice: when the concept is chosen (stage 1, step 4), and at handoff (stage 5, step 2).

## Inputs

- The chosen concept and the course from `scenario.md`; at handoff, also the shots from `storyboard.md`.

## Procedure

### Step 1. Write the sentence

One sentence — what the visitor will say out loud after stepping away from the screen, to someone who hasn't seen it.

It counts only if it contains **an event or an object**:

    "the floor remembered where I walked"
    "I drove into my own name and it fell apart"
    "I stopped at a stop and the sea came up"

None of these count — they are reviews of craft, not events:

    "beautifully made"       "clean typography"
    "moves nicely"           "atmospheric"
    "well-paced rhythm"      "a monumental optical volume"

Craft is the entry requirement, not the achievement. Flawlessly made emptiness scores zero.

### Step 2. Locate it

Name the course step (`K`) and, at handoff, the shot (`S`) where the punch happens. If it happens nowhere in particular, it is a mood, not a punch.

    P   "I stopped at a stop and the sea came up"      ← K3, S5

### Step 3. The subtraction test

Mentally remove the punch. **The work must fall apart** — become meaningless, not merely simpler. If what remains is a decent page, the punch was decoration.

> From the author's practice: one piece, "The Recording Floor", passes. Remove the trail recording and what remains is a grid with a wave — a screensaver. Ten earlier pieces fail: remove any single technique and exactly the same thing remains, just a little calmer.

    without P: a tram line with four labelled stops — a nice diagram, nothing happens. PASS (it falls apart)

### Step 4. Count

There is never a second punch: the second always steals attention from the first (I5). If you have two, keep the stronger and give the other to the next piece — a ready-made continuation of the line.

### Step 5. Check it against the main visitor

The punch must be reachable by `V1` within their time budget, and it must not stand between them and the basics (I2). A punch that only a patient visitor ever sees is a third-minute discovery, not a punch.

    V1 budget 20–40 s; P reachable in ~10 s (one scroll to Low Tide + 1.5 s of stillness). PASS

## Output

- `P` in `scenario.md → ## Punch` (stage 1) and in `DIRECTION.md → ## Punch` (stage 5).
- The `punch` line in `LINES.md`.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] One sentence containing an event or an object, not an evaluation
- [ ] It happens at a named `K` (and `S` at handoff)
- [ ] The subtraction test passes: without it, the work falls apart
- [ ] Exactly one punch (I5)
- [ ] `V1` reaches it within their time budget, and it does not hide the basics (I2)
